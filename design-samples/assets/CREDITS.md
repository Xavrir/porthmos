## Looked at and rejected (files deleted)

- "Reefer containers" (Commons, CC0): the container carries a large carrier logo; a page would read it as an endorsement.
- "Satellite image of Singapore (Landsat 7), 2000": mostly cloud; the strait does not read.
- "MSC Paris, berthing at BCT Gdynia, timelapse" (CC BY 3.0): opens on a photographer's logo slate.
- "Singapore and Malaysia at Night" (NASA): darker duplicate of the ISS picture kept above.
- Not downloaded: "Bill of Lading for Ninety-three Slaves" (1850) came up in the bill-of-lading search and must never be used.
