Proof,then pay.

Porthmos locks an importer’s payment on-chain and releases it to the supplier in three milestones, each after its proof is verified.

One order, three legs

An importer locks 100,000 USDC for an order. The supplier can see it is there. Each leg is paid only when its proof is verified.

  • 20,000handed to the carrier
  • 40,000arrived, in range
  • 40,000quality accepted
  • 0to anyone not registered

The order on this page, PO-0417, is a sample. The milestone module, TradeFlow, is in design. The treasury it runs on, and every refusal named below, is live on Base Sepolia today.

Then someone asks the agent to pay elsewhere.

Not representable.

Today the contract has two actions: deposit into an approved vault, and redeem to your Safe. TradeFlow adds a third: release a leg to the supplier you registered for this order. None of them takes a new receiver.

This exact attack was tried on Base Sepolia (E4c). It was held and logged as ledger row 1, and no transaction exists.

These answers come from a small matcher in this page that applies the published rules. The live agent runs in the app.

When to leave

The Exit Agent uses no AI. When the price moves more than 1% off the peg, the oracle is older than 180 seconds, or a vault can release less than 1.2 times our position, it redeems to the Safe and quarantines the vault. On Base Sepolia we set the price to 0.985, and it left five blocks later.

Porthmos is Greek for a strait: a narrow passage of water, a ferry crossing, guarded on both shores. Thucydides called the sea between Rhegium and Messina simply ho porthmos, the strait. Money in a trade crosses such a passage: from the importer, past proof, to the supplier, with a bank on the far shore.
